About Innopack Automation
"Innopack Automation" Production units are very well placed in the Ambattur Industrial area in the cosmopolitan city of Chennai, India.
Innopack Designs, Manufactures and Supplies the state of the art Packing machines, On-line Check Weighers and Automation Solutions for a wide range of products to all FMCG Industries as well for sectors such as flour, Dal, Rice, Lub Oil, Detergents, Sweets, Bakery, Vermicelli and Macaroni.
FOR OVER TWO DECADE we are in the machinery manufacturing industry particularly in the field of Manufacturing  SPM' S (specialpurpose machines) for Automobile industries and recently diversified in to the manufacturing of packing machines ,check weigher's, automation solutions etc and exporting , under the headship of Innopack Automation Team which has grown by leaps and bounds through its dedication .
To know more  about our SPECIAL PURPOSE MACHINE manufacturing division kindly visit ...www.acmaa.net
Over these years, Innopack  has won the trust of its clients from a gamut of industries. With limitless passion to quality excellence, Innopack is one of the leading supplier of Weighing Machines to many of the top companies in India and Abroad. With seamless efforts to sustain product superiority, Innopack has implemented diverse techniques in the spheres of managerial and process optimization.
